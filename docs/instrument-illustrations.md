## Prompt commun

Square photorealistic studio instrument catalog photograph for a French music school, coherent set.
Seamless matte very pale blue-white background, soft diffused studio light from upper left,
subtle natural contact shadow, elegant restrained educational catalog style. Entire instrument
visible with generous margins, centered, fills 75 percent of image extent. Authentic standard
instrument proportions and mechanically plausible construction. No people, hands, case,
unrelated props, lettering, logo, watermark or decorations. Neutral background and consistent
lighting essential.
